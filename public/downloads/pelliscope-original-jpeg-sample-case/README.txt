PELLISCOPE ORIGINAL THREE-VIEW JPEG SAMPLE CASE
================================================

This folder contains one complete three-view sample case copied byte-for-byte
from the HawkFranklin website repository:

  /assets/img/gab2/context.jpeg
  /assets/img/gab2/close_up.jpeg
  /assets/img/gab2/angled.jpeg

Included files:

  01-context.jpeg
  02-close-up.jpeg
  03-angled.jpeg

These files are original JPEG assets. They were not converted from WebP and
their image data has not been recompressed or resized for this package.

IMPORTANT
---------

These are illustrative presentation assets. They are not a verified diagnosis
and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or emergency decisions.

Package prepared by HawkFranklin OÜ for PelliScope demonstration use.
